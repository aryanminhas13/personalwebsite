01 / DATA MEETS BASKETBALL

Can the numbers
call the game?

For my NBA predictor, I turned more than 25,000 games into a way to explore matchup probabilities. The idea: take team performance, rest days, and home-court advantage, and see what they can tell us together.

I built the full path from raw data to a React interface, with a Flask API connecting the models to the predictions.
